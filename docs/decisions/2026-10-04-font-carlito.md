# Font: Carlito (committed under fonts/, SIL OFL 1.1)

Carlito is metric-compatible with Calibri (same advance widths), the face most
Word resumes use, so:
- a resume brought over from Word keeps its look, with no visual drift;
- line-break behavior matches what the owner has seen in Word;
- the OFL permits committing the font files.

Source: github.com/google/fonts, `ofl/carlito/` (Regular, Bold, Italic, BoldItalic + OFL.txt).

Rejected: Calibri (proprietary, not redistributable); Times/serif options (one
consistent face is easier to measure, and the default look is the starting point).
